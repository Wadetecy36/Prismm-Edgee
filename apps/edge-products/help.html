Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 3-7 business days depending on your location. Express delivery options are available at checkout.

Is my payment secure?

Yes, all transactions are SSL secured and we offer Escrow payment protection, meaning funds are only released to the supplier once you confirm receipt of goods.

How do I return an item?

You can initiate a return from your Orders dashboard within 14 days of delivery. Items must be in their original packaging.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?

Standard local delivery takes 1-3 business days within major metro areas. Regional delivery takes 3-7 business days depending on merchant location. Express options are available at checkout.

Is my payment secure?

Yes. All transactions are processed through verified direct settlement (Mobile Money or Cash on Fulfillment) upon delivery or pickup verification. Your payment is verified directly with our fulfillment team before order handover.

How do I return an item?

To initiate a return or exchange within 14 days of receipt, contact our support team at prismmedgee@gmail.com with your order reference code. Items must be in original condition with merchant packaging intact.

How do quotes work for bulk orders?

On any wholesale product page, click 'Request Quote' to specify your required volume, timeline, and company details. The verified supplier will respond within 24 business hours.
